## Success Criteria Per MS

1. **Core Frameworks** — 3-7 subsystems mapped with interconnections
2. **Context Application** — 5-10 real-world scenarios
3. **Historical References** — Key people + their contributions
4. **Bible Books** — Definitive reading list per domain
5. **Squad Profile** — Agent personas for potential squad creation
6. **SINAPSE Integration** — Rules, patterns, constitutional amendments
7. **Minimum Output** — 800+ lines, 30+ sources
